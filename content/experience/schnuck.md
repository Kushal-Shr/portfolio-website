## the team and the work

A year-round data engineering internship at Schnuck Markets, the St. Louis grocery chain, from May 2025 to September 2026, working hybrid. The work had two sides: moving data into BigQuery, and making sure the data that got there could be trusted.

## what I built

- **Pipelines.** Python pipelines orchestrated in Apache Airflow, extracting from FastAPI services and loading into BigQuery.
- **Data agents.** Two data agents and a set of automated workflows on Agent Engine. They lifted operational efficiency by 20%.
- **A migration.** More than ten legacy datasets moved from Oracle to BigQuery through automated Talend ETL jobs.
- **Data quality.** Three core legacy datasets validated and cleansed by refactoring the stored procedures behind them, to 100% data quality.
