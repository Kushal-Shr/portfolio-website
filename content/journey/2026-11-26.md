## Quiz: Information Security Project Management and Implementation

- **Planning & Tracking:** Using a WBS to break work into action steps, tracking predecessors/successors and milestones, running gap analyses for trade-off corrections, and closing with a project wrap-up.
- **Design & Procurement:** Waterfall SDLC, selecting tech in physical design vs. configuring it in implementation, DevOps, Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA), and vendor RFPs.
- **Rollout Strategies:** Phased implementation (usually best), pilot (one department first), direct changeover ("cold turkey," risking downtime), and user involvement.
- **Change & Governance:** Lewin's change model (unfreezing, moving, refreezing), bull's-eye prioritization, technology governance, management champions, and trained project managers.
