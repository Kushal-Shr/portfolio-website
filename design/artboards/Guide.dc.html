portfolio-sh — how to get around

This site works like a terminal: you type a command, press Enter, and it answers. You don't need to know Linux. Click any example below to see what it prints, then try it for real in the shell.

Rather not type? Every page is one click away in the tab bar at the bottom.

~ means home

folders end in /

In ls, blue names are folders you can cd into; the rest are files you can cat.

.. means up one level
